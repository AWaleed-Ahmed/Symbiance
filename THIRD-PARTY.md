# Bundled Software

The Windows distribution includes Electron (MIT), Node.js (MIT and third-party notices), ws (MIT), Lucide (ISC), and cloudflared (Apache-2.0).

- Electron: https://github.com/electron/electron/blob/main/LICENSE
- Node.js: https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/main/LICENSE
- ws: https://github.com/websockets/ws/blob/master/LICENSE
- Lucide: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE
- cloudflared: https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/blob/master/LICENSE

Electron's LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html are included alongside the unpacked application. Cloudflare binaries are downloaded from official releases and verified against their published SHA-256 digest. Cloudflare Tunnel uses Cloudflare's network; room participants connect through that service.
